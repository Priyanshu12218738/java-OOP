# Why OOP was needed & Procedural vs OOP

## Problem
Imagine a Swiggy/Zomato-style food delivery app. In procedural programming, you might think in terms of functions such as `orderFood()` and `payBill()`. Before moving to Classes and Objects, identify real-world entities that could become objects in an OOP design.

## Solution
**Object (Entity):** A real-world entity that has its own data/details and can perform actions.

Suitable objects:
- `Vendor` (Restaurant)
- `Delivery_Agent`
- `Food_Item`
- `Customer`
- `Order`

## Key Learning
**Properties (State/Data)** are details belonging to an object:
- `customer_id`, `address` → properties of `Customer`
- `total_bill` → property of `Order`

## OOP Mindset
Instead of thinking only about functions such as `orderFood()` and `payBill()`, identify the real-world entities first, then think about their **state/data** and **behavior/actions**.
